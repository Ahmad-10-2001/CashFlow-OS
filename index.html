Setup and troubleshooting

Set this up

  1. Create a free inbox at agentmail.to

    You get an address like cashflow@agentmail.to. No domain to buy, and it accepts mail from anyone — which is what makes this the one provider that works without one.

  2. Save your addresses below — both of them. The AgentMail inbox and your Gmail address: forwarding keeps the original To header, so the server sees your Gmail address, not the inbox. Either one alone leaves half the mail unmatched.
  3. Copy your webhook URL and paste it into AgentMail under Settings → Webhooks, for the event message.received.
  4. Forward your bank emails to the address. In Gmail: Settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP → Add a forwarding address, and tick "Keep a copy" so nothing leaves your inbox.
  5. Run the test below. It sends a sample alert through the whole chain and a real row should appear under Pending. Do this before trusting it with a real transaction.
